Ranking excavation tickets by damage risk

At Urbint I led a rare-event classification model that ranks 811 excavation tickets from highest to lowest risk of damaging underground gas lines, so damage-prevention teams can send inspectors where damage is most likely. The model reached an AUC of about 0.70; the top 1% of ranked tickets captured 20% of damage incidents, and the top 10% captured 60%.

Role
Led modeling, with Product and Engineering
Users
Utility damage-prevention and field inspection teams
Methods
Rare-event classification, H3 spatial features, KNN joins

The problem

Before digging, excavators file an 811 ("call before you dig") ticket. A gas utility receives far more tickets than its damage-prevention team can visit, and actual damages are rare. The team needs a ranked list, not a yes/no label, to decide where on-site inspection time goes.
